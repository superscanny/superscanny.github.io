Delete your account

Super Scanny for Android (com.superscanny.app)

Fastest way: delete it in the app

Open Super Scanny, go to Profile → side menu → Delete account and confirm. Data on your phone is erased straight away. The server steps below happen when your phone is online and may not all complete if you are offline.

What deleting in the app does

  • Erases the data stored on your phone: profile, preferences, household members, scan history, favourites, shopping lists and settings.
  • Revokes the app's device credential on our server, so it can no longer be used.

What it does not do

  • It does not cancel a subscription. Cancel in Google Play: Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Super Scanny.
  • Our server may keep revoked device records (including the billing identifier) for security, fraud prevention, accounting and legal reasons, as described in the privacy policy.
  • Records held by service providers such as RevenueCat, Google and Sentry follow their own policies.
  • Products you added to the shared database and product reports are not tied to your name or email and may remain.

If you cannot open the app

Email mitec.org@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request". Please note:

  • We do not store your name or email on our server, so we cannot look you up by email address. The reliable way to delete your account is to open the app and delete it there. If that is not possible, tell us in your email and we will explain what we can and cannot remove.
  • We handle requests by hand. We will reply to confirm what we found and what we removed or revoked. This page does not itself delete anything.
  • We aim to reply within 30 days.

Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling the app from your phone also removes the data stored on your phone. It does not revoke the device credential on our server, so use Delete account in the app first if you want that done.
