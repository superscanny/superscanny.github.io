Delete your Super Scanny account

This page applies to the Android and iPhone apps.

Fastest way: delete it in the app

Open Super Scanny, go to Profile → side menu → Delete account and confirm. Data on your phone is erased straight away; the server steps are described below and may not all complete if you are offline.

What deleting in the app does

  • Erases the data stored on your phone: profile, preferences, household members, scan history, favourites, shopping lists and settings.
  • Revokes the app's device credentials on our server, so they can no longer be used. If you signed in with Apple, Sign in with Apple is also revoked.
  • If a creator code was linked to your subscription, that link is removed.

What it does not do

  • It does not cancel a subscription. Cancel in Google Play: Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Super Scanny (iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions).
  • Our server may keep revoked device records (including the account or billing identifier) for security, fraud prevention, accounting and legal reasons, as described in the privacy policy.
  • Records held by service providers such as RevenueCat, Google, Apple and Sentry follow their own policies.
  • Products you added to the shared database and product reports are not tied to your name or email and may remain.

If you cannot open the app

Email mitec.org@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request". Please note:

  • We do not store your name or email on our server, so we cannot look you up by email address. The reliable way to delete your account is to open the app (sign in again if you signed in before) and delete it there. If that is not possible, tell us in your email and we will explain what we can and cannot remove.
  • We handle requests by hand. We will reply to confirm what we found and what we removed or revoked; this page does not itself delete anything.
  • We aim to reply within 30 days.
