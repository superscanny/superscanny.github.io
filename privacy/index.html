Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · Last updated 10 October 2026

1. Overview

Super Scanny ("we", "us", or "our") is a food and cosmetic product scanning app for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. We collect only what is needed to provide the service.

Short version: Your dietary preferences, scan history, and favourites are stored only on your device. Photos you scan are sent for analysis and are not kept. Our servers store only an anonymous device identifier, your Apple account ID if you sign in with Apple, and daily usage counts. We do not sell your data.

2. Information we collect

2a. Stored on your device only

The following information is saved locally in the app using iOS device storage and is never sent to our servers:

  • Display name: the name you choose when setting up your profile
  • Email address: shown in your profile only; not collected or stored by our servers
  • Dietary profile: your allergies, intolerances, diet type (for example vegan or halal), and health goals
  • Scan history: the products you have scanned, including product name, barcode, health score, and scan date
  • Favourites: products you have saved
  • App settings: theme, text size, notification preferences, and similar settings

2b. Stored on our servers

Our backend (hosted on Railway) stores the following minimal information to provide the service:

  • Anonymous device token: a randomly generated identifier stored as a one-way SHA-256 hash. We never store the raw token. It is used to authenticate your requests without identifying you personally.
  • Apple User ID: if you sign in with Apple, we store Apple's anonymous, app-scoped user identifier (a long opaque string such as 000000.abc123...). This lets us recognise your account across reinstalls without exposing your name or email. Apple controls this identifier.
  • Daily scan usage counters: we count how many scans your device performs each day to enforce daily limits. These counters reset daily and contain no product or personal data.
  • Products you add: if you add a missing product, the product details you enter (barcode, name, brand, quantity, category, ingredients, and nutrition values) are saved to a shared product database so other users can see them. These entries do not include your name, email, or any account identifier.

2c. Information we do not collect

  • Stored photos. Photos you take for a picture, label, or shelf scan pass through our server to Google Gemini for analysis and are not saved on our servers.
  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Advertising or device identifiers such as IDFA or IDFV
  • Browsing history

4. Third-party services

Super Scanny uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy, linked below.

Google Gemini AI

When you scan a product, its details (such as name, ingredients, and nutrition values) are sent through our server to Google's Gemini AI service to generate a health score and analysis. When you use a picture, label, or shelf scan, the photo you take is also sent so Gemini can identify the products in it. Your name, email, and dietary profile are never included in these requests. We do not store the photos. Please avoid photographing people or personal documents when scanning.

Google Privacy Policy

Sign in with Apple

If you sign in with Apple, Apple authenticates you and provides an anonymous, app-scoped user identifier. Apple may share a private relay email address. We do not receive your real email unless you choose to share it. Apple's privacy practices govern this flow.

Apple Privacy Policy

Sign in with Google

If you sign in with Google, we receive your display name and email address to create your in-app profile. This information is stored on your device only. We do not send it to our servers.

Google Privacy Policy

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat may collect your device identifier and purchase history to verify your subscription status. RevenueCat does not receive your dietary profile, scan history, or personal health data.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Railway

Our backend API and PostgreSQL database are hosted on Railway. All data stored on Railway is described in section 2b. Railway's infrastructure is located in the United States.

Railway Privacy Policy

Sentry

We use Sentry to capture anonymised crash reports and error logs. Sentry does not receive your name, email, dietary profile, scan history, or product data. Crash reports contain only technical information such as iOS version, app version, and stack traces.

Sentry Privacy Policy

5. Data retention

  • Device tokens expire automatically after 90 days of inactivity (30 days for guest accounts). Expired tokens are permanently deleted from our database.
  • Apple User IDs are kept while your account is active and deleted when you delete your account (see section 6).
  • Daily scan counters reset every 24 hours. Historical counters are not kept.
  • On-device data is kept until you delete the app or delete your account.

6. Your rights and account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the app: Profile → side menu → Delete account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your Apple User ID and device token from our servers. On-device data (scan history, dietary profile, favourites) is also erased from your device. This cannot be undone.

To request manual deletion or to ask about your data, email mitec.org@gmail.com.

7. Children's privacy

Super Scanny is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Data transfers

Our servers are located in the United States (Railway infrastructure). By using the app, you consent to your device token and, if applicable, your Apple User ID being processed in the United States. We protect this data in transit using TLS encryption.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify you in the app. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
